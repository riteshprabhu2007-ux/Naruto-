Naruto Uzumaki

Character Information

Naruto Uzumaki is the main protagonist of the Naruto series. He is a ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village and dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his village. Naruto is known for his cheerful personality, determination, and strong sense of justice. He possesses the Nine-Tails Fox spirit sealed within him, which grants him immense power.

Abilities

  • Superhuman Strength
  • Speed and Agility
  • Shadowclone Jutsu
  • Rasengan
